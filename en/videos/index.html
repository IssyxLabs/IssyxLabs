Technical and organizational measures
Commitment: We apply appropriate risk measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or accidental loss.

1 Technical measures

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS enforced)
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 in Google Workspace and Make.com
  • Authentication de dos factores (2FA) en todos los accesos admin
  • Segmentation de data por cliente (client_id unique)
  • API Keys en variables de entorno cifradas, nunca en code
